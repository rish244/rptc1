Who We Are

RP TRANSPORT COMPANY (RPTC)

WE DELIVER SAFE. — that is our promise.

RP TRANSPORT COMPANY (RPTC) is a fleet owner and transport contractor based at D 5103, Bima Complex, Near Steel Market, Kalamboli, Navi Mumbai – 410218, Maharashtra, India. We arrange reliable truck transportation for businesses, traders, manufacturers and distributors across India.

From our base in Navi Mumbai — one of India's key logistics hubs — we coordinate open body trucks, container trucks, light and medium commercial vehicles and heavy multi-axle vehicles for full truck load (FTL) and part truck load (PTL) requirements.

Every consignment is handled with a simple, transparent process: understand the load, match the right vehicle, confirm the booking, and keep the customer informed until delivery.
