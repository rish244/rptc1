What We Offer

Transport Services for Every Load

From a single part-load consignment to full industrial transport contracts — RP TRANSPORT COMPANY arranges the right vehicle, the right route and the right handling for your goods, anywhere in India.

Full Truck Load (FTL)

A dedicated truck is assigned exclusively for your consignment — the goods travel directly from pickup to delivery without trans-shipment. FTL is the fastest and safest option for full-capacity or high-value loads.

  • Exclusive truck for your goods
  • Direct pickup & delivery
  • Ideal for 5–25 ton loads
  • Faster transit, minimal handling
Book FTL

Part Truck Load (PTL)

Don't have a full truck? Share truck space with other consignments moving on the same route and pay only for the space and weight you use. A cost-effective solution for small and medium businesses.

  • Pay only for your load share
  • Regular city-to-city movements
  • Suitable for 1–5 ton consignments
  • Tracked booking reference
Book PTL

Container Transportation

Sealed container trucks (20 ft / 24 ft / 32 ft) protect your goods from weather, dust and pilferage — ideal for electronics, packaged goods, exports and long-distance moves.

  • Sealed, weather-proof containers
  • 20 ft / 24 ft / 32 ft options
  • Best for high-value goods
  • Suitable for rail-road & port moves
Book Container

Open Body Truck Transportation

Open body trucks are the workhorses of Indian logistics — perfect for construction material, steel, cement, sand, bricks, agricultural produce and other weather-tolerant loads.

  • 7.5 T to 25 T options
  • Easy crane / loader access
  • Ideal for bulk & loose material
  • Tarpaulin cover on request
Book Open Body

Heavy Goods Transportation

Multi-axle heavy commercial vehicles for machinery, plant equipment, transformers, industrial components and other oversized or overweight consignments — handled with proper planning and route checks.

  • Multi-axle HCVs up to 25 T
  • Route & load planning support
  • Crane / hydra coordination
  • Permit guidance for ODC loads
Book Heavy Vehicle

Industrial Material Transportation
